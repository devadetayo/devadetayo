ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Cody.

I'm a web developer and product builder who enjoys turning ideas into clean, useful digital experiences. I care about the details, but I also care about getting things working in the real world. Most of my work sits somewhere between frontend engineering, interface design, and building the systems that connect the two.

Outside of code, I play football regularly — it's less about competition for me and more about the reset. A few hours on the pitch clears my head better than anything else, and honestly some of my best problem-solving happens right after a match, not during a coding session.

WHY I DO WHAT I DO

Building things that actually feel right

I got into development because I wanted to do more than write code that technically works. I wanted to understand how an idea becomes something people can actually use — from the structure and logic behind it to the interface they interact with. Building my own projects has given me the freedom to experiment, make mistakes, rethink decisions, and develop a stronger sense of what makes a product feel complete.

I care about the full picture — not just "does it work" but "does it feel right." That's why I own design decisions as much as engineering ones. Most of my time goes into building products and tools that solve problems I actually care about. That includes a real-estate platform spanning mobile and web, as well as AtomAttr, my attribute-based styling system for building interfaces more consistently.
