My Tech Stack.

How it all started.

I started building websites in 2018. I was fascinated by the idea that I could create something that people could interact with on the internet. I started with simple HTML and CSS, and over time, I learned JavaScript and backend technologies. The more I learned, the more I realized how much I enjoyed the process of building and problem-solving.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to work on a variety of projects, each teaching me something new about the craft of building for the web. I've learned to appreciate the balance between functionality and aesthetics, and how a well-thought-out user experience can make all the difference.

Today, I continue to explore new technologies and techniques, always striving to improve my skills and create better experiences for users. My journey as a developer is ongoing, and I'm excited to see where it takes me next.

What keeps me going

What keeps me going is the thrill of solving problems and creating something from nothing. The feeling of seeing a project come to life, knowing that I built it from the ground up, is incredibly rewarding. I also find inspiration in the community of developers and designers around me, who constantly push the boundaries of what's possible on the web.

I am also motivated by the impact that my work can have on others. Whether it's a small personal project or a larger application used by thousands, knowing that my work can make someone's life easier or more enjoyable is a powerful driving force.

I also admire kevin powell, kyle cook of web dev simplified, this people have inspired me to keep learning and growing as a developer. Their content has helped me understand complex concepts and has encouraged me to experiment and try new things in my own projects. and my own family and one special friend has been a source of support and encouragement since we met early this year, reminding me to stay focused and keep pushing forward even when things get tough.
